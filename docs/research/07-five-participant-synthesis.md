## Case-pattern register

The same causal chain requires matching M, P, and I codes. New post-study mechanisms are exploratory and cannot satisfy the 3/5 gate.

A tool ecosystem is defined by the primary storage-and-review mechanism, not by brand count. An Anki-centered workflow and a notes-plus-tutor workflow are different ecosystems; two differently named flashcard applications with the same path may not provide meaningful contrast. Freeze ecosystem definitions before session one and log later changes as protocol deviations.

- **Recurring case pattern:** O/A1/A2-supported in at least two participants.
- **Cross-ecosystem case pattern:** observed in at least three participants across at least two tool ecosystems.
- **Isolated signal:** one participant only; retain it but do not use it to define V0.
- **Stated preference:** keep separate; never promote it to a behavioral pattern without evidence.
- **Counter-pattern:** a low-friction successful workflow, deliberate rejection of resurfacing, or valuable specialist separation. Give it equal visibility.
