# Moderator One-Page Cheat Sheet

Source of truth: [Contextual Observation Protocol](../designs/contextual-observation-protocol.md)

## Non-negotiables

- Observe recent behavior and real artifacts. Ask “show me” before “tell me.”
- Participant operates their tools. Do not tour features or complete tasks for them.
- Never pitch a product, show a prototype, ask “would you use,” or request feature ideas.
- Treat deliberate non-capture and smooth specialist workflows as valid counter-evidence.
- Never combine N and H evidence into one chain.

## Opening script — 0:00–0:04

> Thank you for showing me how you already learn. I am studying the workflow, not evaluating your language ability. I will ask you to show a recent real example and may ask what happened before or after it. There is no product demo. Please hide or skip anything private. You can pause or stop at any time. Is it okay for me to take notes? Is it okay to record the screen or audio for research analysis?

Record note and recording consent separately.

## Anchor selection — 0:04–0:08

**N:** Open the newest authentic-content activity; work backward from the stopping point to the first noticed item. If unavailable, use the newest captured item and log the limitation.

**H:** List every dated capture/correction/highlight/annotation/bookmark history. From each, take the newest eligible artifact aged 7–30 days; choose the newest timestamp across histories. Tie-break: timestamp precision → alphabetical surface name → topmost visible item. Do not cherry-pick an outcome.

Eligible H = one discrete target-language word, expression, sentence, or pattern the learner marked/saved, or one discrete original speaking/writing error with its correction. A raw content-history entry without evidence of a noticed item is ineligible. Minimum evidence: dated original artifact plus current state or dated later event.

Keep a descendant in the H lineage only when an artifact or direct observation connects it to the original.

No eligible H = unevaluable historical chain. Unknown is not positive or negative.

## Session flow

| Time | Focus |
|---|---|
| 0:08–0:20 | Encounter → decide → capture/ignore → prepare; record every handoff and context change |
| 0:20–0:31 | Review → production → correction → reuse; request visible historical evidence |
| 0:31–0:38 | Show how the last study session began and what happened after the last visible step |
| 0:38–0:43 | Replay sequence; verify observed vs reconstructed vs recalled; ask for exceptions |
| 0:43–0:45 | Permission for one follow-up; stop recording before informal conversation |

Thirty-minute version: 5 minutes consent/selection, 20 minutes on one H chain with known outcome, 5 minutes verification.

## Neutral probes

- “Where did this begin?” / “What happened next?”
- “Can you show me the artifact from that step?”
- “What moved with you?” / “What did you add, remove, or rewrite?”
- “What information was available there but is not here?”
- “Did this item appear again? What evidence can we see?”
- “What can you see before answering?” / “What counts as successful here?”
- “Please show me the original attempt and what changed.”
- “How did your last study session begin?” / “What happened after this point?”

Ask frequency, time, and cost only after the behavior is shown.

## Evidence labels

O = directly observed  
A1 = dated history/artifact verifies event or change  
A2 = static artifact exists; sequence reconstructed  
S = self-report only

Critical causal links require O or A1. If evidence is missing, record unknown.
